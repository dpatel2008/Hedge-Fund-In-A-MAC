Prices from 2024-10-14 to 2026-10-15. Made 2026-10-16 10:13 EDT.

The short version

  • Following the tracker's exact rules from 2024-11-11 to 2026-10-15, the SEC strategy would have turned $1,000,000 into about $1,574,346 (+57.4%), or +70.2% with the IWM hedge. Worst drop along the way: -16.6% (hedged -9.3%). Over the same time IWM did +14.4% and SPY +28.7%. 1,119 trades, 58% of them winners.
  • Signals that clearly worked: Going concern warning; Dilution filing (S-1, S-3, 424B5); Late filing (NT 10-K / NT 10-Q); Bad-news 8-K.
  • Signals that did not make money after costs: 2+ insiders selling; Good-news 8-K; 13D filed (not traded now); Any 8-K (the +10 points).
  • 26 SEC form type(s) passed the strict test for a new signal (t-stat 3+, both halves, after costs): 424B5 (short), CORRESP (short), 8-K (short), 6-K (short), EFFECT (short).
  • New ideas that passed the strict test: Going concern, SPACs left out; Dilution: 424B5 (shares actually being sold); Dilution: S-1 or S-3 only (no sale yet).
  • Your real signals so far: 1,169 measured since 2026-10-06. That is too early to judge; the lab keeps adding them every run.
  • Stat arb, Pairs (the research paper's method): +1.6% a year before costs, -0.2% after, Sharpe -0.14 -> NO EDGE (did not make money after costs).
  • Stat arb, Stock vs its sector (the version funds use today): +4.0% a year before costs, -3.4% after, Sharpe -0.58 -> NO EDGE (did not make money after costs).

Statistical arbitrage (pairs trading)

Tested from 2025-10-16 to 2026-10-15 (251 trading days; the first 12 months of prices are only used to pick the pairs). Every trade opens and closes at the next morning's open after the signal, pays costs on every leg and 3% a year to borrow the shorted shares. "Per year" is the return on the money each trade ties up ($1 per pair or per stock). Sharpe = return per unit of risk (above 1 is good, above 2 is excellent). Market beta near 0 means it does not depend on the market going up.

How this was measured

  • Each trade opens at the next morning's open after the filing, because that is the first time the tracker can act.
  • Results are compared with IWM over the same days, so a rising or falling market doesn't count as skill.
  • Costs: 0.05% to 1% each way depending on how much the stock trades, plus 3% a year to borrow shares for shorts.
  • Any single move bigger than 100% is counted as 100% in the averages, so one wild stock can't decide a result.
  • The t-stat says how sure we can be that the average isn't luck: 2 means fairly sure, 3 means very sure. Events on the same day are grouped, so a busy day can't fake a strong result.
  • 1st half / 2nd half: the same average for the older and newer half of the period. A real signal should work in both.
  • Two years is one kind of market. More years (paid price data, or WRDS) would show whether a result holds in crashes and in booms.
